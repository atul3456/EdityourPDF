Help & FAQ

How do I compress an image to a specific size?

Go to Image Editor → Compress Image. Enter your target size in KB or MB and click Compress Now.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF. Output formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP.

Tool not working on mobile?

Use a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari). All tools are touch-optimized.

How do I remove background from an image?

Use Image Editor → Remove Background. Pick the BG color, adjust tolerance (40 works well for white BGs), and download the transparent PNG.

Can I edit PDFs?

Yes! Use the PDF Editor tool to add text, images, erase content, crop, and sign documents.
