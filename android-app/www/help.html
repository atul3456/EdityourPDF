Help Center

Are my files secure?

Yes! 99% of our tools run entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. This means your files never touch our servers, ensuring maximum privacy and security.

The App is not responding?

If a tool seems stuck, try refreshing the page or checking your device's memory. Some large PDFs or Images may take quite a bit of processing power on older devices.

Can I use this offline?

Yes, if you install the native Android APK version, the core editing, combining, and compression features will work completely offline!
